# Arms Race Guide

Arms Race is a daily individual event built around **timing normal account growth into 4-hour scoring windows**. The event is less about spending more resources and more about spending the same resources at the right time.

The practical daily target for most players is simple:

> **Earn 18 Arms Race Badges each day.**

You do not need to complete every phase to do this. Each fully completed phase gives **6 badges** (1 + 2 + 3), so three fully completed phases are enough to reach 18.

---

## 1. How Arms Race Works

Each day is divided into **six 4-hour sessions**. There are five phase types, so one theme appears twice during the day.

The five themes are:

- Hero Advancement
- Base Building
- Unit Progression
- Tech Research
- Drone Boost

The order can vary by server/day. **Check the in-game Arms Race schedule rather than memorizing a universal clock.**

Players are grouped with commanders around a similar progression/HQ range for the daily ranking. Because of that, **phase chest point thresholds and some rewards can vary by account/server progression**.

### Phase badges

Each session has three point chests:

| Chest | Arms Race Badges |
|---|---:|
| First | 1 |
| Second | 2 |
| Third | 3 |
| **Total for a completed phase** | **6** |

Daily badge milestones are:

- **2 badges**
- **8 badges**
- **18 badges**

The highest daily milestone is therefore reached by completing all three chests in **three phases**.

### Do not over-score by default

Unless you are intentionally competing for the daily ranking, stop after the third chest. Resources spent beyond the final phase chest only improve ranking and do not create another phase milestone.

A disciplined player should normally optimize around **18 badges first**, then decide whether the ranking reward is worth additional resources.

---

## 2. The Most Important Arms Race Rule: Stack Events

The best Arms Race action is usually an action that scores somewhere else at the same time.

Alliance Duel / VS overlaps naturally with Arms Race:

- Building actions can overlap with VS building scoring.
- Research actions can overlap with VS research scoring.
- Hero development can overlap with VS hero scoring.
- Troop training/promotions can overlap with VS troop scoring.
- Drone/AP use can often be timed around other PvE events.

Before spending a large stockpile, ask:

> **Can this score Arms Race + VS + another event at the same time?**

This is one of the central resource-management principles of Last War.

## 3. Scoring Reference

The values below are documented from a current Japanese Arms Race guide and should be checked against your own in-game phase because the required chest thresholds scale by progression group.

### Hero Advancement

| Action | Arms Race Points |
|---|---:|
| Legendary/Hero recruitment | **400 per recruitment** |
| Hero EXP | **1 point per 2,000 EXP consumed** |
| Buy a pack containing Diamonds | **30 per Diamond** |

A documented HQ21-22 example uses chest thresholds of approximately:

- First chest: **2,000 points**
- Second chest: **4,000 points**
- Third chest: **12,000 points**

That means recruitment alone would require:

- 5 recruits for the first chest
- 10 recruits for the second chest
- 30 recruits for all three chests

### Best practice

**Recruitment tickets are generally the cleanest way to score Hero Advancement.**

Hero EXP works, but reaching 12,000 points from EXP alone would require roughly **24M Hero EXP**, so dumping XP purely for Arms Race is usually poor resource discipline.

A strong compromise when conserving tickets is:

1. Use 10 recruitment tickets to reach the second chest quickly.
2. Finish the remaining points only if the third chest fits your broader development/VS plan.

## 4. Base Building

| Action | Arms Race Points |
|---|---:|
| Gain 1 building power | **1 point** |
| Reduce construction time by 1 minute | **10 points** |
| Buy a pack containing Diamonds | **30 per Diamond** |

A documented HQ21-22 example uses:

- First chest: **5,000 points**
- Second chest: **10,000 points**
- Third chest: **24,000 points**

### Construction speedup value

If no building-power gain were involved, the third chest would require:

**24,000 / 10 = 2,400 minutes of construction reduction**

That is **40 hours of construction time**.

In practice it usually takes less speedup than this because finishing the building also awards points from the power increase.

### Hold completed buildings when practical

When a completed building remains as a tappable completion/package state, the building power is credited when the completion is collected. This allows a player to prepare a building before the phase and claim the power during Base Building.

This is especially useful for high-power upgrades such as HQ and Tech Center levels.

Do not freeze all construction for long periods just to hold a small completion. The value of continuous account progression can outweigh a minor Arms Race optimization.

## 5. Unit Progression

Unit Progression is one of the most exploitable Arms Race themes because **promoting troops through multiple intermediate levels can score each promotion step**.

### Troop scoring by level

| Troop Level | Points per troop trained/promoted |
|---:|---:|
| 1 | 5 |
| 2 | 6 |
| 3 | 7 |
| 4 | 13 |
| 5 | 15 |
| 6 | 19 |
| 7 | 22 |
| 8 | 25 |
| 9 | 28 |
| 10 | 31 |

Training speedups also score:

- **10 points per minute of training time reduced**

### Waterfall promotion

Do **not** assume directly training the highest troop tier is the best scoring method.

Example for a final Lv.7 troop:

- Train directly as Lv.7: **22 points**

If an appropriate low-level troop already exists and is promoted through:

- Lv.4: +13
- Lv.5: +15
- Lv.6: +19
- Lv.7: +22

Total promotion scoring:

**13 + 15 + 19 + 22 = 69 points**

That is more than **3x the troop-action score** of simply producing the Lv.7 troop directly.

The Japanese test data reports that stepped promotion does **not increase the total final training time or material requirement** versus reaching the same final troop level directly; it simply allows the intermediate promotion actions to score.

### Worked HQ21 example

A documented test used 600 troops and the sequence:

1. Train 600 Lv.3 troops
2. Promote Lv.3 → Lv.4
3. Promote Lv.4 → Lv.5
4. Promote Lv.5 → Lv.6
5. Promote Lv.6 → Lv.7

Recorded result:

- **60,100 Arms Race points**
- **600 net troops added**
- **1,450 minutes of training reduction**

The published HQ21-22 example required approximately **60,000 points** for the full Unit Progression phase.

### Practical setup

Players who care about Arms Race/VS efficiency can deliberately keep barracks at different troop unlock levels so troops can be promoted through several steps.

This should be coordinated with your actual troop needs. Do not cripple account progression just to preserve a scoring trick.

## 6. Tech Research

| Action | Arms Race Points |
|---|---:|
| Gain 1 research power | **1 point** |
| Reduce research time by 1 minute | **10 points** |
| Buy a pack containing Diamonds | **30 per Diamond** |

A documented HQ21-22 example uses:

- First chest: **5,000 points**
- Second chest: **10,000 points**
- Third chest: **24,000 points**

If research speedups were the only scoring source, the third chest would require **2,400 minutes / 40 hours** of research reduction.

In practice, completed research also awards power points, reducing the necessary speedup total.

### Do not idle the Tech Center excessively

You can sometimes hold a completed research and claim it during Tech Research, but unlike a completed building this blocks the research queue.

Usually the better approach is:

- Keep research running continuously.
- Time large completions naturally into Tech Research when possible.
- Use speedups during a VS + Arms Race overlap.

Losing many hours of research production to protect a small Arms Race completion is usually a bad trade.

## 7. Drone Boost

| Action | Arms Race Points |
|---|---:|
| Spend 10 Drone Combat Data | **1 point** |
| Spend 1 AP/stamina | **10 points** |
| Buy a pack containing Diamonds | **30 per Diamond** |

A documented HQ21-22 example uses approximately:

- First chest: **1,500 points**
- Second chest: **10,000 points**
- Third chest: **30,000 points**

### Combat Data

At 1 point per 10 data:

- 15,000 data = 1,500 points
- 100,000 data = 10,000 points
- 300,000 data = 30,000 points

### AP/stamina

At 10 points per AP:

- 150 AP = 1,500 points
- 1,000 AP = 10,000 points
- 3,000 AP = 30,000 points

Obviously, spending 3,000 AP simply to finish Arms Race would be terrible resource efficiency for most players.

Drone Boost is strongest when AP is already being spent on something valuable — for example Gold Zombies, another PvE event, radar activity, or farming needed resources.

**Do not burn AP just because Drone Boost is active. Stack it with a worthwhile AP event.**

---

## 8. Pack Purchases

Most themes can award **30 Arms Race points per Diamond contained in a purchased pack**.

Important distinction:

- The pack must contain Diamonds.
- A purchase without Diamonds does not qualify for this scoring method.

Do not buy packs purely to finish Arms Race unless you were already going to buy them. Treat the Arms Race points as a bonus attached to planned spending.

---

## 9. Stage Swap

Arms Race allows one theme swap per day from the schedule screen.

The swap exchanges the active theme with a later theme. The following move with the theme:

- scoring conditions
- point values
- phase chest progress/status

### When to use it

Stage Swap is useful when:

- a strong VS overlap is available now
- you will be offline during the natural window for an easy phase
- you need to reach 18 badges earlier in the day
- another event makes one phase much more efficient right now

### Important restrictions

Community documentation notes that swapping can be blocked when:

- the target phase has already ended
- there are unclaimed phase chests
- the active phase has less than roughly 5 minutes remaining

The swap is **scheduling flexibility**, not additional scoring opportunities.

## 10. Which Phases Should You Target for 18 Badges?

There is no universal best three because stockpiles and event overlaps matter more than phase names.

A useful priority framework is:

### Usually cheap/easy when prepared

**Hero Advancement**
- Excellent if recruitment tickets are stockpiled.
- 30 tickets can complete the documented HQ21-22 example by themselves.

**Unit Progression**
- Extremely efficient if waterfall promotions are prepared.
- Strong VS overlap.

**Drone Boost**
- Excellent when a good AP-spending event is active or Combat Data needs to be spent anyway.

### Highly dependent on timing

**Base Building**
- Great with prepared completions and construction speedups.
- Especially strong when it overlaps VS building day.

**Tech Research**
- Great with a major research completion and VS research overlap.
- Poor if forcing it requires idling your Tech Center or wasting general speedups.

### Default daily mindset

Do not say:

> “I always complete Hero, Unit, and Drone.”

Instead say:

> “Which three phases can I complete today while spending resources I already wanted to spend?”

That is the correct Arms Race optimization question.

## 11. Resource Preparation Checklist

### Save for Hero Advancement

- Legendary recruitment tickets
- Hero EXP when a large level-up is already planned

### Save for Base Building

- Construction speedups
- Large completed/near-completed buildings when practical
- Major building upgrades for VS overlap

### Save for Unit Progression

- Training speedups
- Lower-tier troops suitable for waterfall promotion
- Barracks with useful staggered troop unlock levels

### Save for Tech Research

- Research speedups
- Major research completions
- Research plans that align with VS

### Save for Drone Boost

- Drone Combat Data
- AP/stamina items
- PvE tasks/events that you already want to run

## 12. Common Mistakes

### Spending the right resource in the wrong 4-hour window

A construction speedup used during Drone Boost creates account progress but throws away potential Arms Race points.

### Chasing rank instead of milestones

Once the third chest is open, additional spending should be a conscious ranking decision.

### Finishing all three chests six times every day

Possible does not mean efficient. The daily gold milestone only requires 18 badges.

### Direct-training high-tier troops during Unit Progression

If waterfall promotions are available, directly training the final tier can leave a large amount of scoring on the table.

### Burning AP with no second purpose

Drone Boost does not magically make a bad AP target efficient.

### Holding queues idle for too long

Timing is useful; stopping account progression for hours to preserve a tiny completion usually is not.

### Ignoring VS overlap

This is often the biggest opportunity cost in the entire event.

---

## 13. Quick Guide

**Daily target:** 18 Arms Race Badges.

- Every phase has 3 chests worth **1 + 2 + 3 = 6 badges**.
- Fully complete **3 good phases** rather than wasting resources trying to finish everything.
- Check the day's schedule because phase order varies.
- Spend big resources when **Arms Race and VS overlap**.
- Hero: recruitment tickets are efficient.
- Building: hold large completions when practical and use construction speedups in the proper window.
- Units: **waterfall troop promotions** for dramatically more scoring.
- Research: time large research + speedups; don't leave the Tech Center idle for long periods.
- Drone: spend Combat Data and AP only when those resources already have another useful purpose.
- Stop at the third chest unless you deliberately want ranking rewards.

> **The goal is not to spend more. The goal is to make every resource score multiple times.**
