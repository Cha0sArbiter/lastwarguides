# Visual Guide Source Hubs

These are broad collection pages rather than individual resources. Item-level manifests should link directly to specific resources whenever possible.

### Last War Tutorial — Community Contributions
- **Source:** https://www.lastwartutorial.com/community/
- **Creator:** Last War Tutorial community
- **Contains:** Community-submitted charts, maps, spreadsheets, calculators, and Discord guides from multiple named creators.

### Last War Helper — Visual Guide Gallery
- **Source:** https://lastwarhelper.com/guides
- **Creator:** Aragantinis #1592 & Scarmoon #1592
- **Contains:** General event guides plus extensive Season 1–4 visual guides.

### REMB — Infographics & Event Guides
- **Source:** https://rembstore.com/pages/event-guides
- **Creator:** REMB
- **Contains:** Progression infographics, Alliance Duel, Desert Storm, Capitol War, Zombie Siege, Radar, stores, and current-event visual guides.

### Cpt Hedgehog — Infographics Library
- **Source:** https://cpt-hedge.com/guides/infographics
- **Creator:** Cpt Hedgehog
- **Contains:** Event calendars, Decorations, Drone Parts, T11, VS rankings, Season 1–6 Black Markets, and map references.

### Cpt Hedgehog — Full Guides Library
- **Source:** https://cpt-hedge.com/guides
- **Creator:** Cpt Hedgehog
- **Contains:** More than 100 guides spanning general mechanics and Seasons 1–6, many with dedicated diagrams and downloadable visuals.

### Last War Tutorial — Main Guide Library
- **Source:** https://www.lastwartutorial.com/
- **Creator:** Last War Tutorial
